Gluten-free, vegan and nut allergies

Gluten-free cakes

Available in vanilla bean, dark chocolate and lemon-poppyseed, in every size, with any filling and frosting. Add $12.00 per cake, or $12.00 per tier on tiered cakes. Gluten-free cakes contain milk and eggs, and the dark chocolate one also contains soy.

They're made with gluten-free ingredients on cleaned pans, but our kitchen uses wheat flour every day, so we can't guarantee they're free of gluten traces. We don't recommend them for people with celiac disease.

Vegan cakes

Available in vanilla bean and dark chocolate, in every size, with marionberry jam filling and vegan vanilla buttercream. Add $10.00 per cake, or $10.00 per tier on tiered cakes. Vegan cakes contain no milk or eggs, but they do contain wheat and soy.

We can't make a cake that is both gluten-free and vegan.

Nuts

Our carrot cake has walnuts and our hazelnut praline cake has hazelnuts. Every other flavor is made without nuts in the recipe, but tree nuts and peanuts are used in our kitchen every day, so we can't promise a nut-free cake.

What we don't make

We don't make sugar-free cakes, and we don't use fondant or edible photo prints. Our decorations are buttercream piping, hand-piped flowers and fresh fruit.

How to order

  1. Choose your cake

    Pick a size by servings, then a flavor, filling, frosting and any extras.

  2. Get in touch at least 72 hours ahead

    Call (503) 555-0147 during opening hours, email cakes@thimbleberry.example.com, or ask at the counter. Tell us:

    • your pickup date
    • how many servings
    • size, flavor, filling and frosting
    • any dietary needs
    • the message for the cake
    • your name and phone number

  3. We confirm your order

    Orders under 25 servings are confirmed when we reply, with nothing to pay until pickup. Orders of 25 servings or more follow the large order steps below.

  4. Pick it up

    Collect your cake at the bakery, Tuesday to Sunday, between 10:00 am and 2:00 pm. Any balance is paid at pickup.

Our decorating bench is small, so we only take a limited number of custom cakes each day. Weekends in May, June and December fill up first, so get in touch as soon as you know your date.

Orders of 25 servings or more

Rosa approves every large order

Any custom cake order that adds up to 25 servings or more, whether it's one big cake or several smaller cakes for the same day, needs Rosa's approval before it's confirmed. She reviews each one herself and replies within 24 hours. Requests sent on a Monday, when we're closed, get an answer by Tuesday at noon.

A 50% deposit holds your date

Once Rosa approves your order, a deposit of 50% of the order total holds your date. Pay it by card over the phone or in person within 48 hours of approval, or the date is released. The remaining balance is due at pickup.

Refunds

Cancel 7 or more days before pickup and we'll refund your deposit in full. If you cancel less than 7 days before pickup, the deposit is non-refundable, because we've already bought ingredients and set aside decorating time.

For example, a 10-inch round serves up to 36 and costs $120.00, so its deposit is $60.00 and $60.00 is due at pickup.
