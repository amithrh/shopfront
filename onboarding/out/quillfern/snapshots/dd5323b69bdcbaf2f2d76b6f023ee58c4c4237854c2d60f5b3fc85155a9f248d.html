Before you book

  • Appointments start every 15 minutes: 10:00, 10:15, 10:30, 10:45 and so on.
  • Color services need a $50 deposit when you book. It comes off your bill at checkout.
  • First time getting color with us, or reacted to hair dye before? Book a free patch test at least 72 hours before your color appointment.
  • Sundays are for cuts, styling and treatments. We don't do color on Sundays.
  • Plans change. Please read our cancellation policy before you book.
