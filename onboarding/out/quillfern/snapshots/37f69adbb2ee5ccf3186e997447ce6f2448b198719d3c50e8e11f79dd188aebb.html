How to book

Call (512) 555-0147 during studio hours
or email quillfernhairstudio@example.com

  1. Tell us the service you'd like (see services & prices) and your stylist, if you have one in mind.
  2. Give us two or three days and times that work for you.
  3. We confirm by email within one business day. For color services, we take the $50 deposit by card at that point.

Need to cancel or move an appointment? See our cancellation policy.
