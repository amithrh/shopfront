Plumbing

Done by licensed plumbers. Hourly work: $125–$145 per hour.

Leak repair

Dripping supply lines, leaking shut-off valves, under-sink connections and visible pipe leaks in copper, PEX or galvanized pipe.

  • Leaks inside a wall or ceiling are tracked down on a $79 diagnostic visit, then quoted.
  • If we open drywall to reach a leak, our handyman team can patch it. Patching is quoted separately.

Water heaters

Repairs for gas and electric tank water heaters, and replacement with a new 40- or 50-gallon tank.

  • Replacements include haul-away of the old tank, new connectors, an expansion tank if code requires one, and the City of Denver permit. We pull the permit, and the permit fee is included.
  • 2-year labor warranty on water heater installs. The tanks we install carry a 6-year manufacturer warranty.
  • Replacements quoted over $1,500 need a 30% deposit when you schedule.
  • No hot water and not sure why? Start with a $79 diagnostic visit.

Drain cleaning

Slow or stopped sinks, tubs, showers and toilets, and main sewer line clogs. We clear drains with cable augers and a main-line cable machine, never with chemical drain cleaners.

  • Main line cleaning needs an accessible cleanout. Without one, we quote the job after a diagnostic visit.
  • We don't excavate or replace sewer lines, and we don't offer hydro-jetting. If the camera shows a broken line, you get the video to share with an excavation contractor.

Handyman

Hourly work: $85–$105 per hour. Scheduled during regular hours only.

Drywall patching

Nail pops, doorknob holes, anchor holes, water-damage cutouts and the hole the plumber left. We match orange-peel and knockdown textures.

  • Medium patches take two visits: one to patch, and one to texture after it dries.
  • We prime every patch. Paint touch-up is included if you have leftover paint.
  • Homes built before 1978: we follow lead-safe work practices as an RRP-certified firm.
  • If we suspect asbestos, for example in a popcorn ceiling, we stop and refer you to a licensed abatement contractor.

  • We break down the boxes and leave them for your recycling, or haul the packaging away for $25.
  • We recommend wall anchoring for dressers and bookcases in any home with kids.

TV mounting

Wall-mounted TVs, level and centered, with cables tidied or hidden inside the wall.

  • Plaster-and-lath walls, common in Denver homes built before 1950, are quoted from photos.
  • We mount TVs up to 85 in. We don't mount on ceilings.
  • The in-wall kit uses a code-approved power bridge, so you don't need an electrician. We don't add new outlets or circuits.

What we don't do

We'd rather point you to the right specialist than take a job we can't do well.

  • Sewer line excavation or replacement
  • Hydro-jetting
  • New gas lines (we connect gas water heaters only)
  • Electrical panels, new circuits or new outlets
  • Furnaces, boilers and air conditioning
  • Roofing and gutters
  • Septic systems
  • Sprinkler and irrigation systems
  • Appliance repair
  • Asbestos or mold removal
