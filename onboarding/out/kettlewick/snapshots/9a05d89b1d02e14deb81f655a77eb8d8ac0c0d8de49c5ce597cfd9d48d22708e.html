How quotes work

We don't book jobs online or schedule anything on the first call. Every job starts with a quote request, so you know the price before anyone comes to your door.

Request a free quote

The four steps

  1. 1

    Send a quote request

    Use the quote form, email quotes@kettlewick.example.com, or call the office and we'll write it up with you. Here's what we ask for:

    What needs doing
    A few sentences about the problem or the job. Brand and model numbers help for water heaters and fixtures.
    Your ZIP code
    So we can confirm you're in our service area.
    Preferred time window
    Weekday morning (arrival 8 am–12 pm), weekday afternoon (arrival 12–4 pm), Saturday morning (arrival 8–10 am), or first available.
    Photos (optional)
    Up to 5 photos, 10 MB each. You can also text them to (720) 555-0147.
    How to reach you
    Text, email or a phone call.
  2. 2

    Jo reviews it and sends a written quote

    Jo Lindqvist, our owner and master plumber, reads every request herself. You'll get one of three replies, by text or email:

    • A fixed price for the job as described. Most faucet, drain, drywall, assembly and TV jobs are priced this way.
    • A free on-site estimate for projects expected to cost more than $1,000, like water heater replacements.
    • A $79 diagnostic visit when we need to find the problem before we can price the fix.

    Quotes are valid for 30 days.

  3. 3

    Approve the quote and pick an arrival window

    Reply to approve, and we'll offer 2-hour arrival windows that fit the time you asked for: 8–10 am, 10 am–12 pm, 12–2 pm or 2–4 pm on weekdays, and 8–10 am on Saturdays. Jobs quoted over $1,500 need a 30% deposit when you schedule.

  4. 4

    We do the work

    Your tech walks through the quote with you before starting, wears shoe covers and cleans up afterward. If we find something the quote didn't cover, we stop, show you, and send a revised quote. We never go over the approved price without your OK. Payment is due when the job is done.

Policies

Quotes
Free, written and valid for 30 days. A quote covers the work it describes. Anything extra gets a revised quote first.
Rescheduling and cancelling
Free up to 24 hours before your arrival window. Inside 24 hours there's a $49 late cancellation fee.
Payment
Due when the job is done. We take credit and debit cards, bank transfer (ACH) and checks. We don't take cash.
Deposits
30% when you schedule, for jobs quoted over $1,500. Payable by card or bank transfer.
Warranty
1 year on plumbing labor, 2 years on water heater installs and 90 days on handyman work. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty. Parts you supply are covered for our labor only.
Someone at home
An adult 18 or older needs to be home at the start and end of every visit. Please keep pets in another room while we work.
Rentals
We work on rental homes when the property owner or manager approves the quote in writing. We bill the owner or manager directly.
Discounts
10% off labor for customers 65 and older and military veterans, up to $100 per job. One discount per job.

Questions we get a lot

Can I book a time online?

No. Every job starts with a quote request. Once you approve the quote, we schedule a 2-hour arrival window with you.

Do you offer same-day service?

Sometimes, for active leaks and no-hot-water calls, when the schedule has room. We can't guarantee it. If water is leaking right now, turn off your main shut-off valve and call our emergency line at (720) 555-0199.

What counts as an emergency?

A leak you can't stop, a burst pipe, no water to the house, sewage backing up, or a leaking water heater. Handyman jobs aren't emergencies for us. They're scheduled during regular hours.

Do you work in Wheat Ridge?

Not at the moment. Wheat Ridge (80033) and Arvada are outside our service area. Call the office and we'll refer you to a licensed plumber who works there.

Is Central Park in your service area?

Yes. Central Park (80238) is in our extended area, so the $35 travel fee applies to each visit.

Do I need to buy the parts?

No. We can supply fixtures and parts at our cost plus 15%, or install ones you've bought. Parts you supply are covered by our labor warranty only.

Can I send photos by text?

Yes. Text them to (720) 555-0147 with your name and ZIP code, or attach up to 5 to the quote form.

Do you work weekends?

We schedule work on Saturday mornings, with arrivals from 8 to 10 am. On Sundays and holidays we handle plumbing emergencies only, with a $195 surcharge.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Jo holds Colorado Master Plumber License MP.0048213, the company holds Denver Plumbing Contractor License PC-27715, and we carry $2,000,000 in general liability insurance plus workers' compensation.

Can you do the job in Spanish?

Yes. Elena, one of our plumbers, speaks Spanish, and we can write your quote in Spanish too.
