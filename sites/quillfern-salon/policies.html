Booking

  • Appointments start every 15 minutes (on the hour, at :15, :30 and :45) and are booked in 15-minute blocks.
  • You can book up to 8 weeks ahead.
  • Every appointment has to finish by closing time, so longer services need an earlier start. For example, balayage past the shoulder blades takes 4 hours, so on a Saturday it has to start by 1:00 pm.
  • You can request any stylist. If you don't have a preference, we book the first available stylist who offers your service.
  • We don't do color services on Sundays.
  • We confirm every appointment by email within one business day of your request.

Deposits for color

  • Every color service (root touch-up, all-over color, partial or full highlights, balayage and color correction) needs a $50 deposit.
  • We take the deposit by card when we confirm your appointment, and it comes off your bill at checkout.
  • Color consultations and patch tests are free and don't need a deposit.
  • Whether a deposit is refunded depends on how much notice you give if you cancel. See below.

Cancellations and no-shows

Your appointment time is held just for you, so we ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Moving an appointment counts the same as cancelling it.

When you let us knowWhat happens
24 hours or more before your appointmentNo charge. A color deposit is refunded in full, or moved to your new appointment.
Less than 24 hours beforeWe charge 50% of the booked service price. A color deposit is kept and counts toward that charge.
No-showWe charge 100% of the booked service price.

To cancel or reschedule, call (512) 555-0147 or email quillfernhairstudio@example.com. The time on your voicemail or email counts as your notice.

Running late

If you're more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your service or move your appointment to another time so the next client isn't kept waiting. If you're running behind, call us and we'll tell you what we can do.

Patch tests and allergies

  • A patch test is required if you're new to color with us, or if you've ever reacted to hair dye. It has to be done at least 72 hours before your color appointment. It's free and takes 15 minutes.
  • Our standard permanent hair color contains PPD (para-phenylenediamine). For all-over color and root touch-ups, we also carry a PPD-free color line.
  • Highlights and balayage use lightener, which contains persulfates. We don't have a persulfate-free alternative.
  • Our smoothing treatment is formaldehyde-free.
  • Our finishing oil contains argan oil. If you have a nut allergy, tell us and we'll leave it out.
  • We use nitrile (latex-free) gloves for every service.
  • Fragrance-free shampoo and conditioner are available on request.
  • Please tell us about any allergies or sensitivities when you book.

Kids and teens

  • The kids cut price ($30) is for ages 12 and under.
  • Clients under 16 need a parent or guardian with them for any color service.

Payment and tips

  • We accept credit and debit cards, tap-to-pay and cash. We don't accept checks.
  • Tips can be added to your card payment or left in cash.

Gift cards

  • Gift cards come in any amount from $25 and can be bought in the studio or by phone.
  • They never expire and can be used for any service. They can't be exchanged for cash.

If something isn't right

Tell us within 7 days of your appointment and we'll adjust your cut or color at no charge. You can come back to the same stylist or switch to another one.

Accessibility

  • Our entrance is step-free, from the parking lot behind the building.
  • We have an accessible restroom, and every station has an adjustable hydraulic chair.
  • Service animals are welcome.
