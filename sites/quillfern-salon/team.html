The team

Three stylists, each with their own lane. Ask for anyone by name, or tell us what you're after and we'll match you with the right chair. If you don't have a preference, we book the first available stylist who offers your service.

Inés Calderón

Owner and color director, 16 years behind the chair

Inés opened Quillfern in 2019 after nine years of coloring hair in San Antonio and Austin. She's the person to see for balayage, highlights, gray blending and color correction, and she still keeps a few haircut slots open each week.

Specialties
Balayage, highlights, gray blending, color correction
Also books
Women's cuts, root touch-ups, all-over color, bang trims, color consultations, patch tests, deep conditioning, scalp treatments
Works
Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday

Wren Abernathy

Senior stylist, 9 years behind the chair

Wren cuts with shears and clippers in equal measure: sharp bobs, pixies, tapers and fades. Wren also handles single-process color and our smoothing treatments, and is happy to tidy up a beard while you're in the chair.

Specialties
Precision cuts, bobs and pixies, men's cuts and clipper work
Also books
Women's cuts, men's cut & beard trim, kids cuts, bang trims, blowouts, root touch-ups, all-over color, color consultations, patch tests, smoothing treatments, deep conditioning, scalp treatments
Works
Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday

June Okafor

Stylist, 6 years behind the chair

June specializes in curly and coily hair, cut dry and curl by curl so you can see the shape as it happens. She also does blowouts, event styling and updos, and she's our go-to for kids cuts.

Specialties
Curly and coily cuts, blowouts, updos and event styling
Also books
Women's cuts, men's cuts, kids cuts, bang trims, deep conditioning, scalp treatments
Works
Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday
