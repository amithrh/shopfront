A Denver repair company that prices the job before it starts.

Kettlewick started in 2014 with one van, one plumber and a list of Sunnyside neighbors who were tired of surprise invoices.

Our story

Jo Lindqvist spent eleven years with larger plumbing outfits before starting Kettlewick. The work was good. The pricing wasn't: customers got an hourly guess on the phone and a very different number on the invoice.

So Kettlewick was built around one rule. You see a written price before we start, and the invoice matches it. If we open a wall and find something nobody could have known about, we stop and send a new quote. Every job still works that way.

Today we're six people with four vans. Most of our work is in Denver's older homes: 1920s bungalows, 1950s ranches with galvanized steel water lines, and plaster walls that don't behave like drywall.
