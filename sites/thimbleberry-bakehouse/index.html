Baked this morning, around the corner.

Thimbleberry Bakehouse is a small neighborhood bakery in Southeast Portland. Every morning we're open, we bake naturally leavened bread, flaky pastry and cookies, and we make custom cakes to order for birthdays, weddings and everything in between.

Cakes made for your table

Choose a size by how many people you're feeding, then pick a flavor, filling and frosting. Every custom cake is baked and decorated to order.

  • Order at least 72 hours before pickup.
  • Gluten-free and vegan cakes are available in select flavors.
  • Orders of 25 servings or more are approved by Rosa and need a 50% deposit.
  • Pickup only, Tuesday to Sunday, between 10:00 am and 2:00 pm.
