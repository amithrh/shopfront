Getting here

Street parking is free on SE Thimbleberry Street and SE 32nd Avenue. There's a bike rack right by our door.

The entrance is step-free, and there's room inside for strollers and wheelchairs.

Dogs are welcome at the bench out front. Inside, only service animals, please.
