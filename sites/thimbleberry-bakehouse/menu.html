About allergens

Everything is made in one kitchen that handles wheat, milk, eggs, soy, sesame, tree nuts (almonds, hazelnuts and walnuts) and peanuts. Each item below lists what it contains, but we can't guarantee anything is free of traces of the others. Items marked gluten-free are made with gluten-free ingredients, not in a gluten-free kitchen.
