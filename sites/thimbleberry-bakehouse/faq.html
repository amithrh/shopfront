Allergens and diets

Is your kitchen nut-free?

No. We use tree nuts (almonds, hazelnuts and walnuts) and peanuts every day. Even when a recipe has no nuts in it, we can't guarantee it's free of nut traces.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes: the gluten-free sandwich loaf ($12.00), the flourless chocolate crinkle cookie ($3.50), and gluten-free custom cakes in vanilla bean, dark chocolate or lemon-poppyseed (add $12.00 per cake, or per tier). All of them are made with gluten-free ingredients in a kitchen that uses wheat flour every day, so we can't guarantee they're free of gluten traces and don't recommend them for people with celiac disease.

What's vegan?

Our country sourdough, seeded rye, olive & rosemary loaf and baguette are vegan, along with the vegan blueberry muffin and the ginger molasses cookie. Vegan custom cakes come in vanilla bean or dark chocolate (add $10.00 per cake, or per tier).

Can you make a cake that's both gluten-free and vegan?

No, we can't make a cake that is both.

Do you make sugar-free cakes?

No, we don't make sugar-free cakes.

Custom cakes

How far ahead do I need to order a custom cake?

At least 72 hours before pickup. We take a limited number of custom cakes each day, so weekends can fill up earlier than that.

Can I get a cake for tomorrow?

Not a custom cake. Most days we have 6-inch case cakes in vanilla bean or dark chocolate ($42.00, serves up to 12). They're first come, first served and can't be reserved.

What if I need a cake for a big party?

Orders that add up to 25 servings or more need Rosa's approval. She replies within 24 hours. Once she approves, a 50% deposit is due within 48 hours to hold your date, and the balance is due at pickup. See large orders for the details.

Can I get my deposit back?

Yes, in full, if you cancel 7 or more days before pickup. If you cancel less than 7 days before pickup, the deposit is non-refundable.

How do I change or cancel a smaller order?

Orders under 25 servings don't need a deposit, and you pay at pickup. Call or email at least 48 hours before pickup to change or cancel.

Do you deliver?

No. All cakes are picked up at the bakery, Tuesday to Sunday, between 10:00 am and 2:00 pm.

Do you do fondant or photo cakes?

No. We don't use fondant or edible photo prints. We decorate with buttercream piping, hand-piped flowers ($15.00) and fresh seasonal fruit ($12.00).

How should I store my cake?

Buttercream cakes are fine at room temperature for up to 4 hours; after that, keep them in the fridge. Cakes with cream cheese frosting should stay refrigerated. For the best texture, take any cake out of the fridge 1 hour before serving.

Visiting and paying

Can I reserve bread?

Yes. Call or email by 2:00 pm the day before and we'll hold your loaves. You pay when you pick them up.

How can I pay?

Cash, credit and debit cards, and tap to pay. We don't accept checks. Oregon has no sales tax, so menu prices are what you pay.

Where can I park?

Street parking is free on SE Thimbleberry Street and SE 32nd Avenue, and there's a bike rack by the door.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome at the bench out front. Inside, only service animals, please.
